JOB CONDITIONS

About the Role

We are looking for passionate Open-Source Contributors and Systems Developers (Rust, C, Python, or FPGA/RISC-V enthusiasts) to join our core engineering community.

Note: MacroMag is currently a non-commercial, community-funded open-source collective (supported via Open Collective). This is an unpaid, volunteer/open-source contributor role ideal for engineers who want to build low-level systems from scratch, gain hands-on RISC-V/kernel experience, and co-create an open hardware-software standard.

What You Will Work On

Depending on your skills and interests, you can contribute to one or more of the following areas:

  • Microkernel Development: Writing clean, memory-safe low-level code in Rust and C for our 64-bit RISC-V microkernel prototype.
  • Virtualized Object Shell (VOS): Designing and implementing the object-oriented shell, sandboxing mechanisms, and execution runtime.
  • Emulation & Tooling: Expanding our existing Python Proof-of-Concept architecture emulator (MacroMag-32) and building testing pipelines.
  • Hardware & FPGA Prototyping: Assisting with RISC-V board testing, FPGA validation, and modular hardware specifications.

What We Are Looking For

  • Practical experience or strong interest in Rust, C, or Python.
  • Familiarity with operating system concepts (kernels, memory management, virtualization, sandboxing) or RISC-V architecture.
  • Experience with Git/GitHub workflows and collaborative open-source development.
  • Self-motivation and enthusiasm for digital privacy, air-gapped computing, and modular hardware.
  • (Bonus) Experience with QEMU, Linux kernel modules, or FPGA development.

What We Offer

  • 100% Remote & Flexible: Contribute from anywhere in the world on your own schedule.
  • Clean-Slate Architecture: Freedom to shape core design decisions without legacy code or corporate bloat.
  • Public Portfolio & Recognition: Full credit as a core contributor on GitHub, Open Collective, and LinkedIn.
  • Hardware Access (Future Grants): As our Open Collective funding grows, community grants will be used to supply contributors with 64-bit RISC-V development boards and FPGA hardware for testing.

How to Apply / Get Started

No formal CV or cover letter is required! To get involved:

  1. Click Apply or send a direct message to Adrian Morawski (Founder) with a brief note about your tech stack and interests.
  2. Explore our repositories and pick up an open task in the Issues tab on GitHub: https://github.com/MacroMag-os
